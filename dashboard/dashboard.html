Help & troubleshooting

How to download a single image

Hover a pin on Pinterest and click the red Download button. On pin pages, paste the URL in the popup for a quick download.

How to download a board or search results

Open the board or search page, click Scan current page in the dashboard, review the grid, and click Download selected. Scroll the page to load more pins before scanning for larger collections.

Videos

Videos exposed as a direct MP4/WebM file can be saved. Segmented HLS (m3u8) streams and DRM-protected content cannot be saved as a single file — this is a documented limitation, not a bypass.

Pinterest requires login

Some boards and full-resolution images are only accessible to signed-in users. Log in to Pinterest in this browser before scanning. PinVault never stores your credentials or cookies.

Nothing is detected

Reload the Pinterest page (Pinterest may have updated its DOM), log in if needed, and re-scan. If the problem persists, report it with the extension version shown in the header.

Where do files go?

Files are saved through Chrome's downloads system to your configured Chrome download folder, optionally under a PinVault subfolder.

Is my data shared?

No. Everything is local. Read the privacy policy in docs/privacy-policy.md.
