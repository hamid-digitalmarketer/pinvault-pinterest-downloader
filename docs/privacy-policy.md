# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 21, 2026

Pinvault Pinterest Downloader ("the Extension") helps you download images and videos from Pinterest for personal use. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Extension does and does not process.

## 1. Data Collection

The Extension **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data**. There is no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no trackers, and no server-side logging.

## 2. Local Processing

All download operations are performed **entirely inside your browser, on your device**. The images and videos you download are saved directly to your computer using your browser's native download mechanism. The Extension cannot access files outside the download flow initiated by you.

## 3. Permissions

The Extension requests only the permissions required for its core functionality (for example, access to Pinterest pages to detect downloadable media). Permissions are used strictly for the purpose described in the Chrome Web Store listing and never for data collection.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services. It only interacts with Pinterest pages that you voluntarily visit.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
